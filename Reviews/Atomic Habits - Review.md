# Atomic Habits - Review

> [!summary] Verdict
> Practical, well-structured framework for building habits. Best when applied, not just read.

## Pros
- Clear four-law framework with actionable steps
- Strong examples and stories
- Identity-based approach is durable

## Cons
- Core ideas repeat across chapters
- Some anecdotes feel padded

## Who should read it
- Anyone building routines or breaking bad habits

## Who should skip it
- Readers who already know habit-formation basics well

## Recommendations
- Read alongside: *Tiny Habits* (B.J. Fogg)
- If you liked it, try: *Deep Work* (Cal Newport)
